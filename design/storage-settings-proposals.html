A / RECOMMENDED

A settings card,
not a dashboard.

The same hierarchy, margins and quiet rows as the surrounding controls.

01One headline

6.97 MiB answers “how much disk?” Allocated space is secondary, rather than another competing total.

02Combine related facts

Counts sit with recovery data. Eight payload rows become three understandable groups and a small composition graph.

03Detail on demand

Exact accounting, shared references and reusable space remain available in one disclosure.

Trade-off
Less visual drama, but the closest fit to the existing settings page.

B / VISUAL EXPLANATION

Two questions.
Two clear answers.

Use separate visual treatments to explain why file size and recovery payload size differ.

01Where disk space goes

The ring makes the dominant category obvious: other data files, not the session database.

02What is retained

A quieter inset panel brings together history counts, payload composition and the non-additive explanation.

03No misleading “free” slice

This is composition, not capacity. It does not imply a disk quota or combine overlapping archive references.

Trade-off
Stronger visual explanation, but taller and less consistent with ordinary settings rows.

C / STRUCTURAL CHANGE

Put evidence beside
the decision.

Merge Automatic recovery and Disk usage. Keep Undo memory separate: RAM limits are not disk limits.

01Counts beside controls

“5 retained” belongs next to the archive limit, not in a disconnected statistics tile.

02Preserve familiar controls

Existing label/value alignment, sliders and switches. A compact storage summary replaces the three headline boxes.

03Do not invent utilisation

The screenshot does not provide exact policy-accounted usage. No “157 KiB / 512 MiB” progress bar based on overlapping references.

Trade-off
Best consolidation, but changes information architecture and makes the policy card longer.
